AIMScribe Annotator

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

AIMScribe Annotator is a research tool that lets a small team correct transcripts of recorded doctor–patient consultations. This page explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google tells the app your email address, your name and your profile picture. The app uses them only to identify you, show your name to the project's admins, and decide which files you may open. Team members are not asked for access to their Google Drive or any other Google data.

Recordings and annotations

  • Recordings, machine drafts and the corrections you make are stored in the Google Drive of the project owner, in folders the owner chooses. The owner gives the app access to that Drive so it can read those files and save the team's work there. The app uses this access for nothing else.
  • Audio you play is downloaded into your browser's memory and is not saved on your device.
  • Corrections you have not yet saved are kept in your browser's local storage until they reach Drive, then removed.
  • The app also keeps, in the owner's Drive, a list of team members (email, name, role) and which files are assigned to whom.

Who can see your information

  • The project's admins see your name, email, role, the files assigned to you and your progress, including time spent annotating.
  • Your annotations can be seen by admins and by reviewers assigned to the same file. Other annotators of the same file cannot see them.
  • The information is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Service providers

The app runs on Cloudflare (hosting) and uses Google (sign-in and Drive storage). They process data only to provide those services. The app has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. It contains your email and name, signed so it cannot be altered. Signing out deletes it.

Google API data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Ask the project lead to remove you. Your access ends immediately and your details are deleted from the team list. Annotations you made belong to the research project and stay in the owner's Drive unless the project lead deletes them. You can also remove the app from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: write to the project lead at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
